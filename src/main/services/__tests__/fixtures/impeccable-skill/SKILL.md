This skill gives you the tools and permission to create design that earns to be called out-of-distribution craft.

Core principles:
- Go all out. No hedging, no shortcuts.
- Dream big and bold. Distinct, beautiful, outstanding and highly inspiring work.

## Setup

1. Run `<skill-base-dir>/scripts/impeccable context` once per session, keeping cwd at the user's project.
2. Load the request's playbook from the Commands table.
3. Load reference/craft-floor.md immediately before editing UI.

## How to design

- **The brief wins.** Honor pinned aesthetics, eras, materials, fonts, and palettes.
- **Refinement preserves; redesign replaces.**
- **Visual authority is evidence, not a filename.**

## Modes

The mode names what the visitor's success looks like on this surface.

- **Persuade:** the visitor decides and acts; design is the product.
- **Operate:** the visitor completes a task. Scanability and consistency outrank expression.
- **Read:** the visitor understands something. Structure for comprehension.
- **Experience:** the visitor is inside the work itself.
